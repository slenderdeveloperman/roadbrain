# ROAD BRAIN

**Behavioral conditioning disguised as brain rot.**

ROAD BRAIN is a short-form public-information transmission system for Indian road behaviour. It takes extremely simple driving rules, presents them with deadpan institutional clarity, then lets the transmission progressively lose its mind around the violation.

> **DRIVE PREDICTABLY.**

The joke may be complicated. The instruction cannot.

## Current locked decisions

1. ROAD BRAIN behaves like a **glitched institution making transmissions**, not a meme account wearing a road-safety costume.
2. Every instruction remains brutally legible even when the transmission collapses.
3. The recurring master audio is part of the brand grammar and is reused across episodes.
4. The current episode carrier is **25.2 seconds** with five states: clean → pressured → broken → collapsing → calm/correct.
5. **Paper** is the visual/static source of truth. **Cavalry** is the motion system. Finishing should stay lightweight unless a specific episode truly needs a heavier editor.
6. Surrealism belongs to the **violation**. The **correction stays boring**.
7. The core public-facing line is **DRIVE PREDICTABLY.**

## Status

ROAD BRAIN is in active prototype production. EP001 establishes the grammar; EP002 and EP003 deliberately break different parts of the same institutional system.
