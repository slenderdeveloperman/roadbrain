## Dramatic engine

A driver changes lanes without indicating. ROAD BRAIN accepts the implied premise that other road users must read the driver's mind. It sincerely tries to install telepathy into road infrastructure. Its prediction network briefly appears stable, then the driver changes their mind. The institution's branching model collapses under an intent that can change without notice. A single indicator blink restores ordinary understanding.

**Hook:** TELEPATHY REQUEST RECEIVED.  
**Apex:** WHY NOT USE THE LIGHT.  
**Rule:** INDICATE. THEN MOVE.

The system is earnest and bureaucratic throughout. The joke comes from its escalating attempt to solve an unsignalled manoeuvre with an impossible sensing system, not from decorative glitching.

## Visual and audio direction

The psychic apparatus resembles an instructional chart drafted by someone who has heard about a brain but never seen one: arrows, flat circles, labels, dotted lines and branching routes. Keep the cars and offence immediately legible. Avoid polished sci-fi HUDs, neon, holograms and generic psychic energy. Use the existing black, asphalt, white and amber system; red is reserved for prediction failure, green for the momentary false success and normal status.

The audio plan reuses the ROAD BRAIN ident without scoring the psychic escalation cinematically. The planned accents are an indicator tick, scan beep, dry system chirp, increasingly layered clicks and a complete dropout before reset. The correction's indicator click is the satisfying solution. The Cavalry render is picture-only; no soundtrack is attached.
