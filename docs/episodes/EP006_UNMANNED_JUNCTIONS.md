# ROAD BRAIN EP006 — UNMANNED JUNCTIONS

**Concept:** A driver reaches an uncontrolled, unsignalized four-way road junction. The hook asks, **“HOW DO YOU CROSS THIS JUNCTION SAFELY?”** Cars, auto-rickshaws and scooters approach from all four sides; everybody tries to rush through the same gap, occupies the middle, and leaves every approach blocked. The episode then slows the scene down: scan each approach, check whether there is room in the exit lane beyond the junction, and wait behind the line if the vehicle cannot clear it. The flow moves from the mixed-traffic hook to the uncontrolled-junction setup, then teaches slow-and-scan and exit-space checks before showing the deadlock those checks prevent. In the correction, the lead holds through blocked, clearing and clear exit states, yields to traffic from the right, then enters only when both checks are clear. **Status:** twelve canonical editable Paper keyframes, a separate nine-frame simplified alternate, and a ten-frame Right Lane Raja POV alternate in the unified storyboard file. The Raja POV Cavalry pass now animates the full approach, gridlock, staggered rewind to the approach lines, hold, clearance checks and turn; final render remains open. **Format target:** 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, 25.2 seconds / 756 frames for the canonical sequence.

## Story

Hook: **HOW DO YOU CROSS THIS JUNCTION SAFELY?** Freeze the mixed-traffic four-way jam. Reset to **NO SIGNAL. / FOUR SIDES. NO ORDER.** Cars, autos and scooters approach from every direction. The lead slows and scans, then checks the exit lane before entering. Show the bad choice as everyone claiming the same gap: `EVERYONE CLAIMS FIRST.` / `CARS. AUTOS. SCOOTERS. NO WAY THROUGH.` Resolve the rush into `FOUR SIDES. ZERO FLOW. / NOBODY CAN MOVE.` No collision is shown.

The episode's main lesson is to check the space beyond the junction before entering. If the exit lane is occupied and there is no room to clear the junction, wait behind the line. The correction is four simple decisions in order: hold before the line while the exit is blocked; keep holding as exit traffic begins to move; keep holding after the exit is visibly clear; yield to traffic from the right; then enter when the exit and junction are clear. Red, amber and green callouts label only the exit states. The right-of-way frame has one amber cue. Do not compress this into “wait, then go.”

The teaching example is deliberately scoped to an uncontrolled junction with no mandatory traffic signs and roads of equal priority. A vehicle approaching from the driver's right has right of way in this scenario. The correction slows before the junction, yields to that approach, then proceeds only when the way is clear. Traffic control, mandatory signs and major-road priority take precedence where applicable.

Correction: **EXIT BLOCKED? HOLD. / EXIT TRAFFIC MOVES. YOU KEEP HOLDING. / EXIT IS CLEAR. LEAD STILL HOLDS. / CHECK RIGHT. LET THE VEHICLE PASS. / BOTH CHECKS CLEAR. MOVE THROUGH.** Keep the lead behind the line through the exit and right-of-way checks. In this uncontrolled, equal-class example, yield to traffic approaching from the right; show it gone before the lead enters.

Final card: **WAIT FOR SPACE. / EXIT CLEAR. CHECK RIGHT. THEN GO.** Follow signs, signals and road priority.

## Rule basis and scope

The [Motor Vehicles (Driving) Regulations, 2017](https://morth.gov.in/sites/default/files/Motor-Vehicle-Driving-Regulation-2017.pdf), Regulation 9(1), requires slowing when approaching an intersection and prohibits entering when doing so is likely to endanger other road users. Regulation 9(2) gives right of way to traffic approaching from the right, except where an authorized person, traffic lights or mandatory traffic signs regulate the junction, or where a vehicle exits a minor road onto a major road. Regulation 9(3) prohibits entering an intersection when traffic there has come to a standstill. Regulation 15(2) also specifies right of way for the vehicle on the right where two roads are of the same category.

Public-facing copy is a conservative mnemonic, not a verbatim legal quotation. This storyboard concerns a road junction without an officer or active traffic control, not an unmanned railway level crossing. It does not instruct drivers to stop when no stop is required; `SLOW DOWN` and `ENTER ONLY WHEN SAFE` preserve the regulation's conditional safety standard. Follow any applicable signs and traffic control.
