## Strong recurring concepts

### Right Lane Raja
Introduced formally in EP004.

A driver completes an overtake but occupies the right lane indefinitely.

System language:
```text
OVERTAKE COMPLETE?
YES

RETURN LEFT?
PENDING

KINGDOM ESTABLISHED
RIGHT LANE RAJA
```

Correction:
```text
PASS. RETURN LEFT.
```

### Shoulder Entrepreneur
Introduced in EP003.

A driver treats the hard shoulder as newly discovered infrastructure. The joke escalates into fake lane creation and infinite road width.

### One Lane at a Time
A vehicle keeps drifting after the intended target lane has already been acquired.

```text
TARGET LANE ACQUIRED
TARGET LANE LOST
SEARCHING...
VEHICLE HAS DECLINED GEOMETRY
```

### Zebra Crossing = Human Lane
Treat the crossing as a lane whose priority belongs to pedestrians.

```text
LANE TYPE: PEDESTRIAN
WRONG SPECIES HAS PRIORITY
```

### Horn ≠ Emotion
Turn unnecessary honking into a failed system attempt to identify an actual hazard.

```text
OBSTACLE? NO
COLLISION RISK? NO
EMOTION? YES
```

### One Way / Mirror World
A wrong-way vehicle forces ROAD BRAIN to invert its own coordinate system until directional consensus collapses.

## Prioritization rule

Do not choose episodes only by the importance of the road rule.

Prefer episodes that do at least one of these:
- break a new subsystem visually;
- introduce a reusable character / archetype;
- add a reusable motion primitive;
- remain instantly understandable to a non-follower;
- create a highly sendable social observation;
- preserve the fixed carrier/audio grammar without bespoke rescoring.

## Legal guardrail

Season 01 planning is grounded in the **Motor Vehicles (Driving) Regulations, 2017**. Before publication, re-check the exact rule, context, road markings/signage and any exception relevant to the episode.

The joke may simplify. The instruction may not become false.
