## Earlier direction: EP003 mnemonic

EP003 establishes a **3–4-note civic jingle** as the recurring recognition device. The melody is foreground; industrial textures are seasoning. The full [EP003 brief](episodes/EP003_SHOULDER_IS_NOT_A_LANE.md) governs its timing and audio requirements.

The motif must be slightly cheerful, faintly bureaucratic, rhythmically distinctive, and singable after one or two exposures. Candidate sounds include cheap electric keyboard, toy xylophone/bell, municipal PA synth, or clean FM pluck. Suggested rhythmic sketches include `DA — da-da — DA` and `DA-da / DA… da`; an original C5–E5–D5–G5 master was rendered and subsequently removed at the user’s request; see [the render handoff](EP003_RENDER.md). No epic score or ominous horror music.

Preserve the essential melodic contour, rhythm, and approximate motif duration across episodes. Change traffic noise, relay clicks, horns, signal sounds, surrounding instrumentation, and degradation. Never destroy the melodic identity.

| Stage | Motif | Surroundings |
|---|---|---|
| Hook, 0.00–1.55 s | Pristine | Relay click, faint crossing signal, road hum |
| Condition, 1.55–4.90 s | Identical, pristine | Sparse civic texture |
| Social contagion, 4.90–9.80 s | Same melody; slight final-note detuning permitted | Extra tick and clipped horn transient |
| Geometry failure, 9.80–14.70 s | Still unmistakable | Late duplicate note, wrong click subdivision, mild edge bitcrush |
| Collapse, 14.70–19.50 s | Recognizable through chaos | Clipping, desync, octave doubling, overload around the tune |
| Correction, 19.50–23.60 s | Perfectly clean | Almost nothing; optional pleasant low click |
| Rule, 23.60–25.20 s | One pristine resolve | Immediate silence at hard end |

## Current production checks

- First melody exposure is foreground and memorable.
- All repetitions preserve the melodic identity.
- Hook tests replace only the opening; the 1.55–25.20 picture/audio carrier is identical.
- The hard reset removes noise, detuning, and distortion immediately.
- The clean return feels ordinary and satisfying, never heroic.
- A listener can hum the motif after two or three repetitions.
- The final and opening notes support intentional replay.

The last two checks require composed audio and a rendered episode. Static Paper boards cannot verify them.

## Fixed 25.2-second structure

```text
0.0–4.9    CLEAN
4.9–9.8    PRESSURED
9.8–14.7   BROKEN
14.7–19.5  COLLAPSING
19.5–25.2  CALM / CORRECT
```

The current track’s conceptual arc:

```text
clean
→ pressure
→ failure
→ collapse
→ mundane restoration
```

## Sonic identity

The successful direction combines:
- industrial/public-information-system timbres;
- dry mechanical percussion;
- simple electronic motif;
- machine-room texture;
- harsh-trance-style **energy curve** without polished club aesthetics;
- abrupt reset into a clean final state.

Useful phrase:

> **Industrial system failure wearing a harsh-trance skeleton.**

Avoid:
- glossy EDM;
- cinematic heroism;
- sentimental resolution;
- giant festival drops;
- lush pads;
- episode-specific rescoring unless absolutely necessary.

## Why the same track matters

The fixed carrier does three jobs:
1. brand recognition;
2. production constraint;
3. structural timing template for Cavalry.

Every new episode asks:

> What does this road behaviour look like at each of the five existing audio states?

The music should not bend around every episode. The episode should bend around the carrier.

## Diegetic SFX

Episode-specific SFX may sit over the recurring carrier, but should be tiny and functional.

Examples:
- indicator click;
- headlamp relay click;
- detection beep;
- ambulance siren;
- one system chirp;
- a short reset/dropout.

Avoid adding a bespoke choir, riser, impact bed or new musical layer just because a visual event is dramatic.

## Final-state rule

The final correct section must **not** sound triumphant.

It should feel:
- dry;
- stable;
- uneventful;
- slightly warmer if useful;
- mechanically correct.

The payoff is:

> **nothing dramatic happens because the driver behaved normally.**

## Original audio vs trending audio

Current strategic preference: preserve ROAD BRAIN’s original carrier as the primary audio identity rather than replacing it with unrelated trending audio.

Trending audio may improve discovery in some contexts, but not at the cost of the series’ recognizability, pacing or narrative structure.

If platform discovery experiments are run, keep the ROAD BRAIN carrier audible enough that the transmission still has its own sonic identity.

## Audio QC

- Same master track duration across episodes.
- Visual state changes land on the same carrier boundaries.
- No episode-specific sound overpowers the master audio grammar.
- State 4 feels like the peak.
- State 5 is clearly cleaner and calmer.
- Ending is dry enough to loop or cut cleanly.
